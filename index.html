How to play

Works with a keyboard or a thumb — pick whichever you've got.

Keyboard

  • W↑ Accelerate
  • S↓ Brake / reverse
  • AD Steer
  • R Reset your car

Touch

On phones and tablets, four on-screen buttons appear automatically in the bottom corners of the game: steer left/right, brake, and gas. No setup needed.
